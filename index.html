Privacy Policy for Arrow Escape

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Arrow Escape ("we", "our", or "the game") is a puzzle game for all ages. This privacy policy explains how we handle any data.

2. Data Collection

We do NOT collect any personal data.

  • No names
  • No email addresses
  • No location data
  • No user accounts
  • No analytics
  • No third-party SDKs
  • No ads

3. Local Storage

Arrow Escape stores game progress (highest unlocked level, tutorial seen status, etc.) locally on your device using browser localStorage. This data is never transmitted, uploaded, or shared.

4. Third-Party Services

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

5. Children's Privacy

Arrow Escape is designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 or over. Since we collect no data, our game is safe for kids.

6. Changes to This Policy

If laws change or features evolve, we may update this policy. The latest version will always be on this page.
